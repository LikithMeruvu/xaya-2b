XAYA-2B — probabilities, not prose.

A 2B multimodal decision model for bounded choices, ordinal scores, and yes/no probabilities. Supply context, a question, and candidate options, optionally with an image; receive a distribution over the options.

The frozen model's historical JevBench public development result is 148/231 (64.07%), including 50/111 (45.05%) on the Hard tier. On the same documented public subset, Open-Jev-2B scored 64.94% overall and 41.44% on Hard. These are public development results, not a sealed benchmark rank.

Built for decisions such as tool routing, support triage, RAG routing, multimodal classification, and rubric scoring. The demo presents candidate probability bars and highlights the selected candidate.
